# SOMA campaign assets
Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, then exported as optimized web assets.

- `hero-premium.webp`: Premium skincare campaign photograph on white with botanical shadows; SOMA green serum dropper, ivory moisturizer jar and cleanser tube arranged on the right, clear negative space on the left for website copy.
- `serum-premium.jpg`, `cream-premium.jpg`, `cleanser-premium.jpg`, `night-premium.jpg`: Four equal product photographs from a generated 2×2 catalog sheet; centered SOMA packaging, sunlit white stone, green leaves, gold accents and crisp commercial detail.
- `skin-premium.webp`: Editorial beauty campaign portrait of a South Asian woman with natural skin texture, pulled-back dark hair, white linen, soft daylight and a white backdrop. This is campaign imagery, not a customer before/after photograph.

Source PNGs are retained alongside optimized campaign files. Product catalog pricing uses a fixed sample merchandising scale of 100 PKR per original catalog price unit; this is not a live exchange rate.
